The play-by-play

  1. 1

    Within minutes: a text from a real locator

    We confirm we have your request and ask anything that sharpens the match. It's a person, so reply like you would to a person — questions, context, a community you're curious about. If a short call would sharpen the match we'll suggest one; reply TEXT ONLY if you'd rather we never dial. Late-night submits get their text in the morning.

  2. 2

    Today: your request gets read end to end

    A locator weighs all of it — budget, timeline, lifestyle, commute, pets, dealbreakers. This is the part software can't do, and it's why the list holds up. Hunting the best in class? Name the standard and we'll match it.

  3. 3

    Then: we verify everything you'd otherwise take on faith

    This week's quotes, live specials, and how each property is screening right now — confirmed by phone with leasing offices. You've read the reviews; we add the numbers.

  4. 4

    Within 24–48 hours: your short list, built to compare

    Pricing, specials, screening fit, and our honest notes side by side — so you can put it next to your own research and see exactly where each place stands.

  5. 5

    You pick favorites; we set up tours and chase the deals

    Virtual or in-person tours on your schedule, plus every concession, look-and-lease special, and employer discount we can pin down for you.

  6. 6

    When you apply, we're on the paperwork

    List us as your locator and we work directly with the property — verifying the quote you were given, fielding qualifying questions, and heading off the avoidable errors that get applications declined.

  7. 7

    Approved → moved in

    We help you line up electricity, internet, and renter's insurance, and we stay reachable through move-in day.

Our end of the deal

We save you time — the calls, the cross-checking, the stale-listing wild goose chases. We save you money — current pricing and every special we can confirm. And we advocate for you on approvals and application strategy. All free, because communities pay locator fees from their ad budgets when you sign — the same fee wherever you choose, which is exactly why we have no favorite except yours. And to be explicit: no community can pay to appear on your short list, just as none can pay into our rankings.

While you wait: text us any community already on your radar — we'll pull this week's quote and put our notes next to your research. Keep comparing at FortWorthApartmentReviews.com in the meantime.
