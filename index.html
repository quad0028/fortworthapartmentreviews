Asked and answered

What does this cost?

Nothing. When you sign a lease, the community pays a locator fee out of its advertising budget. You never see a bill from us.

I've already narrowed it down to two or three places. Is this still useful?

Especially then. Name them in the priorities step — we'll confirm what each is really quoting this week, tell you what we made of their reviews next to their closest competitors, and flag anything about screening that could trip you up before you pay an application fee.

How fast do I get my options?

Usually within 24 to 48 hours on weekdays. Tight deadline? Say so in the form and we'll move faster.

Why do you ask about credit and rental history?

Because Fort Worth properties screen very differently from one another. The honest version of your situation gets you a list that survives the application — not one that just looks good on paper.

What happens after I hit submit?

A locator on our team reads it — a person, not an auto-responder — and starts pulling your options together. You'll hear from us by phone or email.
